03 · New

Custom Vinyl

Personalize shirts, tumblers, tote bags, and more with custom-cut vinyl. I work with two types, depending on what you're decorating:

HTV

Heat Transfer Vinyl

Applied with heat and pressure — the best choice for fabric like t-shirts, hoodies, tote bags, and hats. Durable through the wash and available in solid colors, glitter, and more.

Adhesive

Adhesive Vinyl

A peel-and-stick vinyl for hard, smooth surfaces — tumblers, cups, laptops, car windows, signs, and walls. Not intended for fabric or the wash.

Not sure which one fits your project? Tell me what you're decorating and I'll recommend the right material.
